# Report Workflow System - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Overview
This guide will help you quickly understand and use the report workflow system in Eziliya.

## 📋 Workflow Summary

```
Office Engineer → Site Engineer → Valuer → Completed
```

**3 Simple Steps:**
1. **Office Engineer** creates initial report with property details
2. **Site Engineer** adds field inspection data and photos
3. **Valuer** reviews everything and creates final valuation report

---

## 👥 For Office Engineers

### Creating a New Report

1. **Login** to your account
2. Go to **Office Engineer Profile**
3. Click **"Select Bank to Create Report"**
4. Choose the bank (e.g., AU Small Finance, Yes Bank, etc.)
5. Fill out the form with:
   - Property details
   - Address
   - Measurements
   - Upload documents/photos
6. Click **Submit**
7. ✅ Report is now sent to Site Engineer!

### Viewing Your Reports

1. On your profile page, click **"Show My Reports & Workflow"**
2. See two tabs:
   - **Pending Reports**: Reports waiting for Site Engineer
   - **Completed Reports**: Finished reports

---

## 🏗️ For Site Engineers

### Processing Pending Reports

1. **Login** to your account
2. Go to **Site Engineer Profile**
3. Click **"Show Pending Reports & Workflow"**
4. You'll see reports from Office Engineers
5. Click **"Process Report"** on any report
6. Review Office Engineer's data
7. Add your field inspection details:
   - Site photos
   - Actual measurements
   - Property condition
   - Boundary verification
8. Click **Submit**
9. ✅ Report is now sent to Valuer!

### What You'll See

- **Pending Reports Tab**: Reports waiting for your inspection
- **Completed Reports Tab**: Reports you've already processed

---

## 💼 For Valuers

### Creating Final Reports

1. **Login** to your account
2. Go to **Valuer Profile**
3. Click **"Show Pending Reports & Workflow"**
4. You'll see reports from Site Engineers
5. Click **"Process Report"** on any report
6. Review all data:
   - Office Engineer's initial report
   - Site Engineer's field inspection
7. Add your valuation:
   - Property value assessment
   - Market analysis
   - Final recommendations
8. Click **Submit**
9. ✅ Report is now completed!

### What You'll See

- **Pending Reports Tab**: Reports waiting for your valuation
- **Completed Reports Tab**: All finished reports with full details

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "No Pending Reports"
- **Office Engineer**: Create a new report
- **Site Engineer**: Wait for Office Engineers to submit reports
- **Valuer**: Wait for Site Engineers to complete inspections

### "Cannot Submit Report"
- Check all required fields are filled
- Ensure you have internet connection
- Verify you're logged in
- Try refreshing the page

### "Report Not Found"
- Report may have been processed by another user
- Refresh the page to see updated list
- Check the Completed Reports tab
